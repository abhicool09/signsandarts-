Eligible cases

Damaged product, not working on arrival, wrong item, or missing parts reported within 7 days of delivery.

Keep proof ready

Replacement dispatch

Replacement shipping starts after request approval and courier processing in Shiprocket.

Replacement Rules

  • Replacement request must be submitted within 7 days of delivery.
  • Product, original packaging, and accessories should be kept ready for courier pickup.
  • Damage caused by incorrect installation, wrong voltage, water exposure beyond product rating, or misuse is not covered.
  • Custom-made orders are reviewed manually and may not be eligible for automatic Shiprocket replacement.
