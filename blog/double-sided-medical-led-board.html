Double Sided Medical LED Board: Why Two Sides Win

Updated July 3, 2026 | Keywords: double sided medical led board, medical two side led board, two side medical board

A double sided medical led board — often searched as a medical two side led board — lights up on both faces instead of one. It sounds like a small detail, but on any two-way road it decides whether half your passing customers ever see your shop. This guide explains when two sides are worth it and when a single side is fine.

Quick answer: A double sided (two side) medical LED board shows the plus or cross on both faces, so traffic from both directions sees it. On a two-way road it doubles who notices your shop — for a small price difference.

How a two side board doubles reach

A single-sided board faces one direction. Everyone approaching from the other side sees only its blank back. A two side medical board shows the lit plus to both streams of traffic — so on a two-way road, twice as many people notice you for only a small extra cost.

Corner shops gain the most

If your medical shop sits on a corner or junction, a double-sided board mounted perpendicular to the road catches traffic from two different streets at once. This is the single best placement for a two-side board and why corner pharmacies almost always choose double sided.

When single sided is enough

If your shop is on a one-way street, or the board sits flat against a wall that only faces one direction, a single-sided board is fine and cheaper. Two sides only pay off when both faces can actually be seen.
