Medical & Pharmacy Green Plus LED Sign Board Guide

Updated July 1, 2026 | Keywords: medical pharmacy led sign board, pharmacy plus led sign, green plus sign board

The green plus sign is the most recognised medical symbol for pharmacies and medical stores in India. Signs and Arts offers two green plus options: the outdoor Medical & Pharmacy LED sign board with remote-controlled flashing, and the Pharmacy Plus LED sign board for a straightforward double-sided pixel cross.

This guide compares both boards to help pharmacy and medical store owners choose.

Quick pick: Choose the Medical & Pharmacy board for outdoor, remote-controlled flashing; choose Pharmacy Plus for a simpler double-sided pixel cross.

Medical & Pharmacy LED sign board

The Medical & Pharmacy board is a double-sided, outdoor-ready green plus sign with ultra-bright pixel LEDs, a weatherproof iron frame and remote control for flashing patterns. Available in 18x18 and 24x24 inch, it is priced from Rs. 2,789 and suits pharmacies and medical stores that want extra visual movement to attract attention at night.

Pharmacy Plus LED sign board

The Pharmacy Plus board is an 18x18 inch double-sided green pixel cross, priced at Rs. 2,689, with the same weatherproof iron frame and plug-and-play setup, but without the remote-controlled flashing feature. It is a good option for stores that want reliable signage without extra controls.

Choosing between the two

If your pharmacy or medical store is on a busy street and you want a flashing, remote-adjustable board, the Medical & Pharmacy sign is the stronger choice. If you prefer a simple, steady green cross without remote settings, the Pharmacy Plus board covers the essentials at a slightly lower price.

FAQs

Which board has remote control?
The Medical & Pharmacy LED sign board includes remote-controlled flashing patterns. The Pharmacy Plus board does not.

What sizes are available?
Medical & Pharmacy comes in 18x18 and 24x24 inch. Pharmacy Plus is available in 18x18 inch.

Are both boards suitable for outdoor use?
Yes, both use a weatherproof iron frame designed for outdoor storefront installation.
