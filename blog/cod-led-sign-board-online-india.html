COD LED Sign Boards in India: Cash on Delivery Explained

Updated June 23, 2026 | Ordering & COD

Many shop owners prefer to pay when the product reaches them. Signs and Arts offers Cash on Delivery (COD) on LED sign boards across India, so you can order online with confidence and pay most of the amount only when your board arrives.

Quick answer: Pay a small advance of ₹200 to confirm your COD order. A ₹50 COD charge is added, and the remaining balance is paid to the delivery person when the board reaches you.

How Cash on Delivery works at Signs and Arts

StepWhat happens
1. Place orderChoose your board and size and select Cash on Delivery at checkout.
2. Pay ₹200 advanceA small advance confirms the order and reserves your stock. A ₹50 COD charge applies.
3. DispatchYour board is packed and dispatched, usually within 24 hours, with tracking.
4. Pay the rest on deliveryPay the remaining amount to the courier when the board is delivered to your shop.

Why we take a small advance

The ₹200 advance keeps orders genuine and helps us reserve ready stock and dispatch fast. It is adjusted against your total, so you are not paying extra — only the ₹50 COD handling charge is added for cash collection. If you would rather pay the full amount online, UPI, cards and net banking are also available through our secure checkout.

Is COD available everywhere in India?

COD is available across most pin codes in India through our courier partners. A few remote locations may be prepaid-only. If you are unsure, send your pincode on WhatsApp before ordering and we will confirm COD availability and the delivery estimate.

FAQs

How much do I pay upfront for COD?
Just ₹200 as an advance to confirm the order, plus a ₹50 COD charge. The rest is paid to the delivery person on arrival.

Can I check the board before paying the balance?
You can inspect the sealed package on delivery. For any issue after opening, contact us on WhatsApp with photos and we will help.

Do you deliver across India?
Yes, we ship pan-India with tracking and dispatch most in-stock boards within 24 hours.
