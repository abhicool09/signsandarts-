Green Clinic LED Sign Board Guide: Choosing Clinic Cross Signage

Updated July 1, 2026 | Keywords: green clinic led sign board, clinic medical led sign board, clinic cross sign board

Clinics and doctor offices across India commonly use a green cross as a universal medical symbol. Signs and Arts offers two ready-made options: the Green Clinic LED sign board, a double-sided green pixel cross, and the Clinic Medical LED sign board, which flashes green and red for extra attention.

This guide compares both so clinics, hospitals and doctor offices can pick the right signage.

Quick pick: Choose the Green Clinic board for a clean single-colour cross; choose the Clinic Medical board for a flashing green-and-red combination that draws more attention.

Green Clinic LED sign board

The Green Clinic board is a double-sided, ultra-bright green pixel LED cross available in 18x18 and 24x24 inch. It has an iron frame and weatherproof build, making it a solid choice for clinics, hospitals and doctor offices that want a clean, traditional medical cross without extra colours.

Clinic Medical LED sign board (green & red)

The Clinic Medical board is a 24x18 inch double-sided board that flashes green and red pixel LEDs, designed for clinics, medical shops and drug stores that want extra visual movement to catch attention on busy roads. It is wall mountable and storefront ready.

Which one should you choose?

If your clinic is on a quieter street or wants a professional, understated look, the single-colour Green Clinic board is a better fit. If your clinic is on a busy main road with heavy competition for attention, the flashing green-and-red Clinic Medical board stands out more from a distance.

FAQs

What is the difference between the Green Clinic and Clinic Medical boards?
Green Clinic is a single-colour green cross in 18x18/24x24. Clinic Medical is a 24x18 board that flashes both green and red for more visual attention.

Are both boards double sided?
Yes, both the Green Clinic board and the Clinic Medical board are double sided.

Are these boards suitable for hospitals?
Yes, both are commonly used at clinic and hospital entrances as well as by individual doctor offices.
