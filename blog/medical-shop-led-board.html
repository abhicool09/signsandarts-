Medical Shop LED Board: How to Choose the Right One

Updated July 3, 2026 | Keywords: medical shop led board, led board for medical shop, medical store led board

A medical shop led board is often the highest-return investment a medical store can make: it works every evening, needs no maintenance beyond a power socket, and brings in walk-in customers who would otherwise pass by without noticing the shop. But there are several types of led board for medical shop use — plus symbols, cross lights and lettered boards.

This guide breaks down the types so you can pick the right one for your store.

Quick answer: For most medical shops, a double-sided plus/cross LED board in 18x18 inch is the best first purchase — it is recognised instantly, runs on a normal socket and starts around Rs. 2,689 ready-made.

Type 1: Plus symbol LED boards

The classic choice. A double-sided plus/cross that hangs above the entrance — red like the Medical Plus board or green like the Medical & Pharmacy board. Best for being spotted from far away; the symbol does the talking. Read more in the medical plus symbol light guide.

Type 2: Lettered medical boards

Boards that spell out MEDICAL in LEDs, like the Medical Red & Green board which combines red MEDICAL lettering with a green cross. Best when nearby shops already have cross boards and you want your category stated explicitly.

Type 3: Custom name boards

A custom LED board with your shop's own name costs more and takes longer to make, but builds your brand. Many shop owners hang a ready-made plus board first (visibility from far) and add a custom name board on the fascia later. See the custom LED sign board guide.

What a medical shop LED board costs

Ready-made boards start around ₹2,689 (18x18 green plus) and range up to about ₹6,819 for large double-sided lettered boards. All ship pan-India with plug-and-play setup and COD available — full pricing in the LED sign board price guide.
