Medical Plus Symbol Light: The Complete Guide for Medical Shops

Updated July 3, 2026 | Keywords: medical plus symbol light, plus symbol medical shop light, plus sign medical store

A medical plus symbol light is the single most recognised sign for a medical store in India. The glowing plus (cross) shape tells customers from far away that medicines are available here — no text needed. Whether you search for a plus symbol medical shop light, a medical shop plus light or a plus sign medical store board, they all refer to the same product: an LED cross-shaped light board for the storefront.

This guide explains the types, colours, sizes and prices of plus symbol lights so you can pick the right one for your medical shop.

Quick answer: A medical plus symbol light is an LED cross-shaped board mounted outside a medical shop. Ready-made boards come in 18x18 and 24x24 inch, in red or green, and are plug-and-play with pan-India delivery.

What is a medical plus symbol light?

It is a cross-shaped LED board, usually built on an iron frame with rows of ultra-bright pixel LEDs forming the plus symbol. Most boards are double sided so the plus is visible from both directions of the road, and they flash or chase in patterns to draw attention at night. A medical store plus led light typically runs on a standard 230V socket and is plug-and-play.

Red plus vs green plus: which colour?

Both colours are widely used in India. A red plus — like the Medical Plus LED Sign Board — has the highest contrast at night and is common for medical and multi-speciality shops. A green plus — like the Medical & Pharmacy Green Plus board or the Pharmacy Plus board — is the traditional pharmacy colour. If nearby shops already use green, a red plus can help you stand out, and vice versa.

Sizes and mounting

Ready-made plus symbol lights come in 18x18 inch and 24x24 inch. The 18x18 size suits normal shop frontages; the 24x24 size is better for main roads and wider viewing distances. Boards mount with a hook — hang from a rod, chain or bracket above the shop entrance so the plus faces road traffic from both sides.

FAQs

What is the price of a medical plus symbol light?
Ready-made plus symbol LED boards start around Rs. 2,689 for an 18x18 inch green plus, with larger 24x24 inch and remote-controlled options costing more.

Should I choose a red or green plus light?
Red offers the strongest night contrast and suits medical shops; green is the traditional pharmacy colour. Pick the colour that stands out from neighbouring shops.

Is the plus light visible from both sides of the road?
Yes, most ready-made plus symbol boards are double sided, so the cross is visible to traffic coming from both directions.

Does a plus symbol light need an electrician?
No, these boards are plug-and-play on a standard 230V socket and hang from a simple hook mount.
