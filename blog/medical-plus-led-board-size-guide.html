Medical Plus LED Board Size Guide: 18″×18″ vs 24″×24″

Updated June 23, 2026 | Size Guide

Choosing the right size is the most common question before buying a medical plus or pharmacy LED board. Signs and Arts boards come in two main sizes — 18″×18″ (about 45×45 cm) and 24″×24″ (about 60×60 cm). The right one depends on how far away customers see your shop and how much wall space you have.

Quick answer: Pick 18″×18″ for narrow lanes, indoor counters and shops on busy footpaths where people pass close by. Pick 24″×24″ for wide roads, first-floor clinics, or when the board must be read from across the street.

How to choose your size in 30 seconds

Stand where your customers usually stand — the bus stop, the opposite footpath, or the road. If that distance is roughly the width of a normal road or less, 18″×18″ is enough. If your shop is set back, on a first floor, or on a wide highway, go for 24″×24″ so the plus symbol stays clearly readable.

Does size change brightness or features?

No. Both sizes use the same ultra-bright pixel LEDs, the same weatherproof iron frame, double-sided display and plug-and-play 230V power. The larger size simply has a bigger lit area and more LEDs, so it reads from farther away. Both are equally suitable for outdoor use — see our outdoor waterproof guide.

FAQs

Which is the most popular size?
18″×18″ is the most popular for medical stores and pharmacies on regular streets. 24″×24″ is chosen for wide roads and first-floor visibility.

Can I mount a 24″×24″ board myself?
Yes. Both sizes are plug-and-play and come with a hook, nut and bolt for self-installation on a wall or ceiling.

Is the 24″ board double the price of the 18″?
No. The larger size costs more but not double. Check the live price on the product page or ask us on WhatsApp.
