LED Sign Board Price in India (2026)

Updated June 23, 2026 | Pricing Guide

The price of an LED sign board in India depends on the board category, size, whether it is single or double sided, and whether you buy a ready-made board or order a custom one. Ready-made boards have a fixed price because the design is already produced, while custom acrylic or 3D boards are usually charged per square foot, which makes them more expensive and slower to deliver.

Quick answer: Ready-made LED sign boards from Signs and Arts start at ₹1,829 for smaller shop boards and go up to around ₹6,819 for larger double-sided medical and optical boards. Pixel LEDs for building your own sign start at ₹165.

What affects LED sign board price?

Size: an 18″×18″ board costs less than a 24″×24″ board of the same design. Sides: double-sided boards cost more than single-sided because they have LEDs on both faces. Build: weatherproof iron-frame boards for outdoor use are priced higher than basic indoor boards. Ready vs custom: ready-made category boards are cheaper because the design is standard, while custom name or logo boards are made to order.

Why ready-made boards are cheaper than custom

Custom acrylic and 3D-letter boards are usually quoted per square foot and need design, fabrication and longer delivery. A ready-made medical, pharmacy or clinic board skips all of that — it is already produced and dispatched within 24 hours, so you pay a fixed, lower price.
