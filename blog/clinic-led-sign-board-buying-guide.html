Clinic LED Sign Board: The Complete Buying Guide for Doctors

Updated July 3, 2026 | Keywords: clinic led sign board, clinic sign board, doctor clinic sign board

A clinic led sign board helps patients find your clinic the first time, without phone calls and without walking past. Unlike retail shops, clinics depend on trust — so the board needs to look professional, not flashy for its own sake. This clinic sign board guide covers the main options for doctors, from the classic green cross to speciality boards.

Quick answer: A double-sided green cross clinic board in 18x18 inch suits most clinics; choose 24x24 or a flashing green-and-red board for main-road locations, and a speciality board (NEURO, DENTAL) if you practise a specific field.

The classic: green cross clinic board

The Clinic LED Sign Board and the Green Clinic board use the green cross — the most trusted symbol for medical services. Double sided, weatherproof iron frame, available in 18x18 and 24x24 inch. This is the right default for general practitioners and family clinics.

For busy roads: flashing green & red

The Clinic Medical board (24x18 inch, double sided) flashes green and red pixel LEDs. Choose this when your clinic sits on a busy main road where a steady sign gets lost among other lit boards. Comparison in the green clinic board guide.

Speciality clinic boards

If you practise a specific field, a speciality board tells patients they have found the right doctor: Neuro Plus for neurologists, Dental Plus for dentists, Optical Plus for eye care. Each uses the same medical cross format with the speciality lettered on the board.

Placement and etiquette for clinic signage

Mount the board above the entrance, perpendicular to the road if double sided. Keep it at a height where the full cross is visible over parked vehicles (roughly 8-10 feet). For clinics inside residential buildings, check society rules on signage placement before installation.

FAQs

Which sign board is best for a doctor's clinic?
A double-sided green cross board in 18x18 inch is the standard choice; speciality boards (NEURO, DENTAL, OPTICAL) suit single-speciality practices.

What size clinic board should I buy?
18x18 inch for normal streets and building entrances; 24x24 inch or the 24x18 flashing board for main roads and wider viewing distances.

Are clinic LED boards weatherproof?
Yes, the ready-made boards use weatherproof iron frames built for outdoor mounting.

How is the board installed?
It hangs from a hook mount on a rod or bracket and plugs into a standard socket — no electrician required.
