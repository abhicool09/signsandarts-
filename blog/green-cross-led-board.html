Green Cross LED Board: The Pharmacy & Clinic Signage Standard

Updated July 3, 2026 | Keywords: green cross led board, pharmacy green cross light, green plus led board

The green cross led board is the international symbol of a pharmacy — customers everywhere associate a glowing green cross with medicines. In India, chemists, medical stores and clinics use green cross boards to be found quickly, especially at night when a lit cross is visible far beyond the reach of any painted board.

This guide covers the green cross options available, their differences and how to choose.

Quick answer: A green cross LED board is the universal pharmacy symbol. Ready-made double-sided boards start around Rs. 2,689 in 18x18 inch, with 24x24 inch and remote-controlled flashing options available.

Why the green cross works for pharmacies

The green cross needs no translation and no reading — it communicates "medicines available" to anyone at a glance, in any language. For shops in mixed markets or on highways, this matters: a customer on a moving two-wheeler recognises a green cross in under a second.

Green cross board options

The Medical & Pharmacy Green Plus board (from ₹2,789) is double sided with remote-controlled flashing patterns, made for outdoor storefronts. The Pharmacy Plus board (₹2,689) is a simpler steady double-sided green cross. The Green Clinic board uses the same green cross language for clinics and doctor offices.

Size and placement

Both 18x18 and 24x24 inch sizes are available. Hang the board perpendicular to the road above your entrance so both faces work — that is the point of a double-sided board. Corner shops benefit most, catching traffic from two streets at once.

Green cross vs green cross with text

A plain cross is cleaner and readable from farther away. If you also want your category spelled out, boards like the Medical Red & Green board combine lettering with a green cross. For a full comparison of medical signage types, see the medical cross light board guide.
