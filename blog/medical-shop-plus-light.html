Medical Shop Plus Light: The Board Every Medical Store Needs

Updated July 3, 2026 | Keywords: medical shop plus light, plus board for medical shop, medical store plus light

Ask any pharmacist what one sign a new store needs, and the answer is a medical shop plus light — the illuminated plus that tells the whole street "medicines available here." A plus board for medical shop use pays for itself quickly because it captures walk-in customers who'd otherwise never notice the shop, especially at night.

Quick answer: A medical shop plus light is a glowing plus board that marks your store as a medical shop. It's the first sign most stores buy — double sided, plug-and-play, from Rs. 2,689 with COD.

Why the plus light is the first sign to buy

Before a custom name board, before a fascia sign, a medical store plus light does the essential job: it makes the shop findable from a distance. Customers looking for medicines scan the street for the plus, not for your shop name. Get the plus up first; add branding later.

Which plus light for your shop?

For a standard medical store, the red Medical Plus board or the green Medical & Pharmacy board (remote-controlled flashing) are the top picks. A budget-friendly steady green cross is the Pharmacy Plus board at Rs. 2,689.

Placement for maximum walk-ins

Hang the plus light perpendicular to the road, above the entrance, high enough to clear parked vehicles (around 8-10 feet). Double-sided boards catch traffic from both directions — essential on two-way streets and unbeatable on corner shops.

Cash on Delivery and setup

Plus light boards ship pan-India with Cash on Delivery available (small advance, balance on delivery). Setup is a hook mount plus a power socket — most owners install it themselves in half an hour.
