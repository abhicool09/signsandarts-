Medical LED Sign Board Guide: Red & Green Double Sided Signage

Updated July 1, 2026 | Keywords: medical led sign board red green, medical store led board, drug store sign board

The Medical LED sign board in red and green combines bold red MEDICAL lettering with a bright green pixel LED cross. This dual-colour design gives medical shops, pharmacies, clinics and drug stores both clear text readability and the universal medical cross symbol in one board.

This guide explains sizing, build quality and where this design works best.

Best for: Medical shops, pharmacies, clinics and drug stores that want strong contrast lettering plus a recognisable green cross.

Why red text with a green cross works well

Red lettering has high contrast and is easy to read from a distance, while the green cross is the symbol customers associate with medical stores across India. Combining both means the board communicates through text and symbol at the same time, useful for customers scanning a row of shops quickly.

Sizes: 18x18 vs 24x24 inch

Available in 18x18 and 24x24 inch, double sided. The larger size is recommended for medical shops on wide roads or with more distance between the storefront and passing traffic.

Build quality

The board uses a weatherproof iron frame suitable for outdoor installation, with plug-and-play setup and pan-India delivery.
