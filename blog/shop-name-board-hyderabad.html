Shop Name Board in Hyderabad: Materials, Sizes and Pricing Factors

Updated May 27, 2026 | Shop Name Boards

A shop name board is often the first thing a customer notices. In Hyderabad, streets are crowded with competing boards, so your board needs readable text, proper lighting and a clean installation style.

Quick answer: For a unique shop name, choose a custom board. For common categories like medical, pharmacy and clinic, ready-made LED boards can be faster and more economical.

Material options

Common options include ACP, acrylic, glow sign boards, 3D letters, LED pixel boards and vinyl-backed boards. The right choice depends on budget, look and visibility.

How size is decided

Width of frontage, road distance and height from eye level decide board size. A small board on a wide road will not perform well.

What to avoid

Avoid too many colors, tiny text, low contrast and poor mounting. A simple board with strong readability usually performs better.

FAQs

Which shop name board suits a small storefront?
Acrylic letters or a compact LED name board usually work well. For main roads, glow sign or ACP boards give stronger visibility.

Do you make boards with my exact shop name?
Yes. Custom shop name boards are made in Hyderabad based on your text, logo, size and storefront photo.

When should I choose a ready-made LED board?
Choose ready-made boards when the category sign matters most, such as MEDICAL, PHARMACY, CLINIC, DENTAL, XEROX or BAR.
