Pharmacy Sign Board in India: Green Plus LED Board Guide

Updated May 27, 2026 | Pharmacy Boards

A pharmacy sign board should be visible, familiar and clear. The green plus symbol is one of the easiest ways to help customers identify a medicine store, especially at night or in a crowded market.

Quick answer: A green plus LED board is the safest ready-made choice for pharmacy shops. Use a custom board if your pharmacy has a strong brand name or a large storefront.

Why green plus boards work

The plus symbol is instantly associated with healthcare and medicine. A bright LED plus sign helps the shop stand out without needing long text.

Single side or double side

If your shop faces only one road, a single front-facing board can work. If customers approach from both directions, double-sided boards perform better.

Installation tips

Install the board at a height where it is visible above parked vehicles. Avoid hiding it behind shutters, wires or other boards.

FAQs

What is a practical pharmacy sign board?
A green plus LED board is a practical ready-made choice because customers quickly associate it with pharmacy and medical stores.

Should I choose pharmacy text or medical plus?
Choose PHARMACY text when the shop name needs that exact category. Choose medical plus when symbol recognition matters more from the road.

Are pharmacy boards available in two sizes?
Most plus-style pharmacy boards are available in 18 inch and 24 inch options depending on stock and product model.
