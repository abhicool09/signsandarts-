Medical Light Board: How Bright Should It Be?

Updated July 3, 2026 | Keywords: medical light board, medical led light board, medical led

A medical light board earns its keep after sunset. A painted board disappears in the dark, but a medical led light board keeps pulling in customers all evening — exactly when people rush out for medicines. The brightness and the type of LED decide how far your board is seen, so this guide focuses on what actually makes a medical light board bright and long-lasting.

Quick answer: A medical light board is an LED-lit board for a medical shop. Ready-made pixel-LED boards stay bright for 30,000+ hours and are visible from far down the road at night — starting around Rs. 2,689.

Pixel LEDs vs strip LEDs: brightness that lasts

The single biggest factor is the LED type. Cheap boards use LED strips that look bright on day one but dim and develop dark patches within months. Quality medical led boards use individual pixel LEDs — each one is brighter, evenly lit, and rated for 30,000+ hours. Every board we sell, including the Medical Plus board, uses pixel LEDs for this reason.

How far will it be seen?

An 18x18 inch pixel-LED medical light board is clearly readable from across a normal road and down the street at night. For a wider main road or a shop set back from the road, step up to 24x24 inch — the larger board roughly doubles the useful viewing distance.

Double sided doubles your reach

A single-sided board only faces one way; a double-sided medical light board is seen by traffic from both directions. On a two-way road this literally doubles who notices your shop. See our double-sided medical board guide for why this matters.

Running cost of a light board

Owners worry a bright board means big electricity bills — it doesn't. An LED medical light board draws roughly as much power as a ceiling fan, so running it every night costs only a few rupees a day while working like a 24/7 salesperson.
