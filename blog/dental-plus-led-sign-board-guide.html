Dental Plus LED Sign Board: Red & White Guide for Dental Clinics

Updated July 1, 2026 | Keywords: dental plus led sign board, dental clinic led board, dental plus board price

Dental Plus LED sign board is a ready-made outdoor signage option built for dental clinics that want a bold, medical-style identity. It uses a red cross body with clear white DENTAL lettering, powered by ultra-bright pixel LEDs so it stays visible from the road even after dark.

This guide covers sizes, pricing, installation and why a Dental Plus board is a fast alternative to a custom acrylic sign for new and existing dental clinics across India.

Best for: Road-facing dental clinics that want an instantly recognisable red cross plus white DENTAL lettering, visible day and night with pixel LEDs.

Why choose a Dental Plus LED sign board

Patients often search for a clinic while walking or driving past busy streets. A dental plus board with a red cross and white lettering registers instantly, even from a distance, because the shape and colour combination is associated with medical and dental services across India. This reduces missed customers and repeated phone calls asking for directions.

Sizes: 18x18 vs 24x24 inch

The Dental Plus LED sign board is available in 18x18 inch and 24x24 inch sizes. The 18x18 size suits single-shop frontages and narrow building entrances, while the 24x24 size is better for main-road clinics, larger buildings or locations with more distance between the sign and the viewer.

Build quality and installation

The board uses an iron frame with a weatherproof build, making it suitable for both indoor reception areas and outdoor storefronts. It comes with a hook mount for quick wall installation and is plug-and-play, meaning no electrician visit is required for setup.

Dental Plus vs custom dental signage

A ready Dental Plus board is faster to get and lower cost than a fully custom sign with your clinic name. If you need your clinic name, doctor name or logo added, a custom illuminated board is the better route — see our dental clinic signage guide for customisation options.
