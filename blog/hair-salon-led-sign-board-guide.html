Hair Salon LED Sign Board: Signage Guide for Salons & Grooming Studios

Updated July 1, 2026 | Keywords: hair salon led sign board, beauty parlour led sign, grooming studio signage

A hair salon led sign board with a scissors graphic and bold HAIR SALON lettering helps salons and grooming studios stand out on busy shopping streets and inside malls. The white scissors icon paired with red lettering is easy to spot and instantly communicates the service on offer.

This guide covers the design, sizing and best placement for this ready-made salon signage board.

Best for: Hair salons, beauty parlours and men's grooming studios that want an instantly recognisable scissors-and-lettering sign.

Design: scissors graphic with HAIR SALON lettering

The board combines a white scissors graphic with red HAIR SALON lettering in an 18x12 inch single-sided indoor format. This combination works for unisex salons, women's beauty parlours and men's grooming studios alike, since scissors are a universal symbol for hair services.

Where to install

Best placed above the salon entrance or reception counter where it is visible from the street or mall corridor. Since it is single-sided, position it facing the main direction of foot traffic.
