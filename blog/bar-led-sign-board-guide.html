Bar LED Sign Board: Multicolor Signage Guide for Pubs, Hotels & Restaurants

Updated July 1, 2026 | Keywords: bar led sign board, pub led sign, bar mugs led sign

A bar led sign board with a multicolor mugs graphic is a popular way for bars, pubs, restaurants and hotels to mark their bar section or entrance. Unlike plain text boards, the mugs graphic instantly signals "bar" to customers browsing a busy street or mall corridor.

This guide covers the design, sizing and installation of the ready-made bar sign board from Signs and Arts.

Best for: Indoor bar counters, pub entrances and restaurant bar sections that want a fun, colourful visual identity.

Design: multicolor LED with bar mugs graphic

The board features a multicolor LED display of clinking bar mugs, an 18x12 inch single-sided indoor format. The bright, playful colour scheme suits nightlife and hospitality venues better than plain white or red lettering.

Where to install a bar sign board

This board works well above a bar counter, at a pub entrance, inside a hotel lounge, or at the bar section of a multi-cuisine restaurant. Because it is an indoor single-sided design, it is best placed where customers view it from one direction, such as a wall behind the counter or above a doorway.

Plug and play installation

The board is plug-and-play, so bar and restaurant owners can install it themselves without hiring an electrician. It runs on standard indoor power and is priced at Rs. 2,189 with pan-India delivery.
