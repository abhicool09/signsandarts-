Open Close LED Sign Board Combo: Guide for Shops & Retail Stores

Updated July 1, 2026 | Keywords: open close led sign board, open close shop sign, retail store open close board

An Open Close LED sign board lets shop owners clearly display their current status to passing customers. Instead of a paper sign taped to the door, an illuminated board is visible from a distance, even at night, and looks far more professional.

This guide explains the difference between the single boards and the combo pack, and how retail stores typically use them.

Best value: The Open + Close combo pack lets you switch between boards depending on shop hours, at a lower combined price than buying separately later.

Single board vs combo pack

You can buy just the Open board or just the Close board individually at Rs. 2,189, or get the combo pack with both at Rs. 3,981. Most retail stores prefer the combo pack so they can switch the visible board to match actual shop hours instead of leaving a static sign.

How shops use Open/Close signage

Retail stores, salons, clinics and small offices commonly place the board at the entrance door or shop window. Staff simply switch which board is powered on or facing outward, based on whether the shop is currently serving customers.

Installation

Both boards are plug-and-play, so switching between Open and Close requires no rewiring — just power the relevant board when needed.
