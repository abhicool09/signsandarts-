LED Sign Board for Pharmacy: What Chemists Should Buy

Updated July 3, 2026 | Keywords: led sign board for pharmacy, pharmacy led sign board, pharmacy sign board

Choosing an led sign board for pharmacy use comes down to one goal: being found by customers who need medicines, often urgently and often after dark. A lit board keeps working when painted fascia boards disappear into the night. This guide covers the pharmacy led sign board options, prices and what matters in the build.

Quick answer: The standard LED sign board for a pharmacy is a double-sided green plus board — ready-made from Rs. 2,689 in 18x18 inch, weatherproof and plug-and-play with pan-India delivery.

The green plus: the pharmacy standard

The green plus is to pharmacies what the red cross is to hospitals. Two ready options: the Medical & Pharmacy Green Plus board (from ₹2,789, remote-controlled flashing, outdoor build) and the Pharmacy Plus board (₹2,689, steady double-sided cross). Both are covered in detail in the green cross LED board guide.

What to check before buying

  • Double sided: a single-sided board wastes half the traffic on your road.
  • Pixel LEDs, not strips: pixel LEDs stay bright longer and flash in sharper patterns.
  • Weatherproof frame: the board lives outdoors year-round — an iron frame with sealed backing is a must.
  • Plug and play: the board should run off a normal socket without wiring work.

Night pharmacies: pair with a 24-hours board

If your pharmacy operates round the clock, add an Open 24 Hours LED board next to the plus sign — late-night customers specifically scan for it. Many night pharmacies run the plus board and the 24-hours board together.

Delivery, COD and installation

Ready-made pharmacy boards ship pan-India with Cash on Delivery available (small advance, balance on delivery). Installation is a hook mount plus a power socket — most owners hang the board themselves in under half an hour.
