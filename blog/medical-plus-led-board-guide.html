Medical Plus LED Board: Best Signage for Medical Shops in India

Updated May 26, 2026 | Keywords: medical plus led board, medical shop plus light, medical sign board

A medical plus LED board is one of the most recognizable signs for a medical store, chemist shop or clinic. When a customer is searching for medicine at night, the green plus symbol and bright LED display help them identify your shop quickly from the road.

This guide explains what to look for before buying a medical shop plus light, why illuminated signage matters, and how to choose between 18 inch and 24 inch boards for your storefront.

Quick answer: For most medical stores, an 18 inch double sided plus LED board is enough for narrow streets and indoor-front shops. For main roads, junctions and larger storefronts, a 24 inch medical plus LED board gives stronger distance visibility.

Why a medical plus light board helps your shop

Storefront signage works like a silent salesperson. Signage research commonly notes that signs identify a business, communicate what it offers and help customers decide where to stop. For medical shops, that recognition is even more important because customers may be looking for urgent medicine, first aid products or late evening pharmacy access.

A good medical light board should do three jobs:

  • Show the plus symbol clearly from the road.
  • Stay visible after sunset and during rain.
  • Make the store look professional and trustworthy.

Best size for a medical sign board

The right size depends on road width and viewing distance. A compact 18"x18" board is suitable for small medical shops, clinics inside markets and local stores. A 24"x24" medical plus sign works better when your shop is on a main road, near a hospital, or among many competing boards.

Simple rule: if customers need to notice the board from moving traffic, choose the larger size. If they mostly walk past your shop, the smaller size can work well.

Single sided vs double sided medical LED board

A single sided board is usually enough when customers see your shop only from the front. A double sided LED medical sign board is better for road-facing shops because people can see it from both left and right directions. That is why plus boards for medical stores are often double sided and wall mounted with a hook or bracket.

What features to check before buying

  • Pixel LED brightness: Choose a board that remains visible in evening light and roadside glare.
  • Weatherproof frame: Outdoor boards should handle dust and rain.
  • Plug and play wiring: A ready board saves installation time.
  • Strong hanging hook: Important for wall mounted boards.
  • Clear plus design: The plus symbol should be readable, not decorative only.
