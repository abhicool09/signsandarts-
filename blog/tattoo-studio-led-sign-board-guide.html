Tattoo Studio LED Sign Board: Signage Guide for Tattoo Parlours

Updated July 1, 2026 | Keywords: tattoo led sign board, tattoo studio signage, tattoo parlour led sign

A tattoo led sign board with bold red TATTOO lettering and a white oval LED border gives tattoo studios and parlours an unmistakable street presence. Since tattoo studios are often located inside shopping complexes or mixed-use buildings, strong, high-contrast signage helps clients find the right entrance quickly.

This guide covers the design, placement and specifications of this ready-made board.

Best for: Tattoo studios and parlours that want a bold, unmistakable storefront identity.

Bold red lettering for instant recognition

The bold red TATTOO text is designed for maximum readability, important in markets where tattoo studios compete for attention alongside salons, cafes and retail shops in the same building.

Board specifications

This is an 18x12 inch single-sided indoor board priced at Rs. 2,189, framed with a white oval LED border, plug-and-play with pan-India delivery.

Placement for studios and parlours

Mount at the studio entrance or reception desk, facing the main walkway. In shared buildings, placing it near the building directory or lift lobby (in addition to the studio door) can help new clients locate the studio faster.
