Spa LED Sign Board: Signage Guide for Spas & Wellness Centers

Updated July 1, 2026 | Keywords: spa led sign board, spa open sign, wellness center signage

A spa led sign board helps spas and wellness centers communicate a calm, welcoming identity while still being clearly visible from the street. The blue SPA text combined with green OPEN lettering and a white oval border gives a soothing colour palette that fits the spa and wellness category better than harsh red signage.

This guide explains the design and where to install this board for the best effect.

Best for: Spas, massage centers and wellness studios that want a calm yet clearly visible entrance sign.

Colour choice: blue and green for a calming effect

Unlike medical or retail signage that often uses red for urgency, spas benefit from cooler tones. The blue SPA text paired with green OPEN lettering signals relaxation and wellness while remaining bright enough to be read from a distance.

Board specifications

The Spa Open board is an 18x12 inch single-sided indoor sign, priced at Rs. 2,189, with a white oval border framing the text for a clean, contained look.

Installation tips

Place the board at the entrance or reception area of the spa, facing the main approach path used by clients. Since it is single sided, avoid placing it where clients would view it from the side or rear.
