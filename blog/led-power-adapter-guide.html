LED Power Adapter Guide: 12V 5A 60W Adapter for Sign Boards & LED Strips

Updated July 1, 2026 | Keywords: led power adapter, 12v 5a led adapter, 60w led power supply

Every LED sign board, LED strip or pixel module needs a matching power adapter to run reliably. The 12v 5a led adapter from Signs and Arts is a 60W indoor power supply designed specifically for LED-based products, including sign boards, strip lights, modules, neon signs and CCTV systems.

This guide covers the specifications and how to know if this adapter fits your setup.

Best for: Powering LED sign boards, LED strips, LED modules, neon signs and CCTV systems that require a stable 12V DC supply.

Specifications

This is a 12V DC, 5A, 60W power adapter with a 2.1 x 5.5 mm centre-positive DC connector, the most common connector size for LED strips, modules and small sign boards in India.

What it can power

Suitable for LED sign boards, LED strip lights, pixel LED modules, neon signs and CCTV cameras that run on 12V DC. Before buying, check the wattage requirement of your specific board or strip length to confirm the 60W capacity is sufficient — for larger installations, multiple adapters or a higher-wattage supply may be needed.

Indoor use only

This adapter is designed for indoor use. If your sign board is installed outdoors, ensure the adapter itself is placed in a protected, weatherproof enclosure rather than exposed directly to rain.
