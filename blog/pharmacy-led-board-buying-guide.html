Pharmacy LED Boards: Green Plus Sign Buying Guide

Updated May 26, 2026 | Keywords: pharmacy led boards, pharmacy sign board, medicine store green plus sign

Pharmacy LED boards are one of the fastest ways to make a medicine shop visible from the road. A green plus sign, bright pixel LEDs and a clear shop category help customers understand that they can buy medicines, first aid and health products from your store.

If you are comparing a pharmacy sign board, pharmacy plus sign LED light or hospital LED sign board, this guide will help you choose the right size and build quality.

Best practical choice: For most Indian pharmacy shops, choose a double sided green plus LED sign if the board faces traffic from two directions. Choose a larger 24 inch board for main roads and a compact 18 inch board for local streets.

Why pharmacy signs need to be simple

People should identify a pharmacy quickly. Complicated graphics can look nice, but they are harder to read from a distance. A simple green plus shape, strong white lettering and bright LED points work better because the message is instant: this is a pharmacy or medicine store.

Retail signage studies and industry resources often focus on visibility, legibility and the ability of signs to identify a business. For pharmacies, these points matter because customers may be searching while walking, driving or standing across the road.

What makes a good medicine store green plus sign?

  • Bright green LEDs: Green is strongly associated with medical and pharmacy signage.
  • Weatherproof frame: Outdoor pharmacy boards face rain, heat and dust.
  • Double sided visibility: Useful when your shop is seen from two road directions.
  • Easy installation: A hook or wall mount saves time.
  • Low power consumption: LED boards can stay on for long store hours.

FAQs

What is the best LED board for a pharmacy?
A green plus LED board with weatherproof frame and bright pixel LEDs is a good choice for most pharmacies.

Is 24 inch better than 18 inch?
Yes for main roads and wider viewing distance. For small lanes, 18 inch can be enough.

Can the pharmacy LED board be used outside?
Choose an outdoor/weatherproof model for rain-facing shop fronts.
