Open 24 Hours LED Sign Board: Signage Guide for Round-the-Clock Shops

Updated July 1, 2026 | Keywords: open 24 hours led sign board, 24 hour shop sign, always open led sign

An Open 24 Hours LED sign board tells customers immediately that a shop is always open, which is especially useful for late-night pharmacies, convenience stores, cafes and restaurants. The board combines green OPEN text, red 24 HOURS lettering and a white LED border for maximum night-time visibility.

This guide covers the design and best use cases for this signage.

Best for: Shops, cafes, restaurants and retail stores that stay open around the clock and want customers to know it at a glance.

Why display 24-hour signage

Customers looking for a shop late at night or early morning often drive or walk past several storefronts before finding one that is open. A dedicated 24 hour shop sign removes the guesswork, reducing missed sales for round-the-clock businesses.

Design and colours

The board uses green for OPEN, red for 24 HOURS, and a white LED border outlining the sign — a combination chosen for strong contrast and quick recognition even from moving vehicles.

Where it works best

Common uses include 24-hour pharmacies, medical stores, convenience stores, petrol pump shops, cafes and restaurants that operate through the night. It is an 18x12 inch single-sided indoor board, priced at Rs. 2,189.
